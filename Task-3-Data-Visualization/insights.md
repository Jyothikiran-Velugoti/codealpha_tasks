# Netflix Data Visualization Insights

## 1. Movies vs TV Shows
- Netflix contains more Movies compared to TV Shows.
- This indicates Netflix's strong focus on movie content.

## 2. Content Added By Year
- Netflix content growth increased significantly over recent years.
- The platform expanded its library rapidly.

## 3. Top Countries
- The United States contributes the highest amount of Netflix content.
- Other countries also contribute to global content diversity.

## 4. Top Genres
- International Movies, Dramas, and Comedies are among the popular categories.

## 5. Ratings
- Most Netflix titles belong to mature audience categories.

## 6. Directors
- Some directors have contributed multiple titles to Netflix.

## 7. Movie Duration
- Most movies have durations between 80-120 minutes.

## 8. Release Year Trend
- Recent years show higher content production compared to older years.
